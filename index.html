29:50

The shrine

Kanda Myojin is an old Edo shrine above Akihabara, and a favourite of anime fans. We take the Ginza Line from Mitsukoshimae to Suehirocho (about 5 minutes), then walk 10 minutes. The grounds are open early and quiet before the shops open.

  • Kanda Myojin on Google Maps
  • Official site

310:15

The bow maker

Koyama Kyugu has made bows for over 200 years and has its own workshop for bows, arrows and gloves. It opened at 10:00, so we arrive to a calm shop. You can hold the bows and ask the staff anything. This is the main stop, so we take our time. It is a 15 minute walk from the shrine.

Animate,Japan's biggest anime shop

From Awajicho station (one minute from the bow shop) it is the Marunouchi Line to Ikebukuro, about 17 minutes with no changes. Animate's main store is about 5 minutes on foot from the east exit: manga, character goods, and anime shops on the streets around it.

Spicy ramen,at Kikanbo

Red miso broth with chili and Sichuan pepper. You choose how hot and how numbing, and you can ask for it milder. It is a few minutes on foot from Animate. There can be a short line at lunchtime.

Pokémon Center,Sunshine City

We walk to Sunshine City, about 15 minutes. The Pokémon Center Mega Tokyo is on the second floor of the Alpa shopping area, the biggest Pokémon store in Tokyo. We have about an hour here.

Metal records,and done

Disk Union has rock and metal records and band T-shirts, new and used. It is about 15 minutes on foot back from Sunshine City, so we only go if we still have time and energy. If not, we finish at the Pokémon Center. Either way we are done around 14:30 in Ikebukuro. Back to Nihonbashi is about 30 minutes: Marunouchi Line to Otemachi, then one stop on the Hanzomon Line. Or we split here.
