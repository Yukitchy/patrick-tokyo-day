210:00

The bow maker

Koyama Kyugu has made bows for over 200 years and has its own workshop for bows, arrows and gloves. We arrive when it opens at 10:00, so the shop is calm. You can hold the bows and ask the staff anything. This is the main morning stop, so we take our time.

On foot to lunch. The five places are 4 to 14 minutes away, and you choose at the next stop.

412:15

Lunch,you pick one of five

All five are in Ikebukuro, and all are open at lunchtime on Tuesday. Tell me which one you feel like and we go. The photos show the dish, not the shop.

Kikanbo

★ 3.6 · Tabelog, 1,800+ reviews

Spicy miso ramen. You choose how hot and how numbing (Sichuan pepper) it is, and you can ask for it mild. Counter seats only, and a short line is normal at lunch.

Open 11:00 to 21:30, every day · about 4 min on foot from Animate

Map ↗ Details ↗Photo: example of the dish. Very hot tsukemen (close-up).jpg by insatiablemunch, CC BY 2.0
Gyukatsu Motomura

★ 3.1 · Tabelog, 23 reviews, opened Feb 2026

Beef cutlet fried quickly so the middle stays rare. You finish each slice on a small hot stone at your table. Inside Sunshine City, in the same Alpa building as the Pokémon Center.

Open 11:00 to 22:00, every day · about 7 min on foot from Animate

Map ↗ Details ↗Photo: example of the dish. Gyukatsu (46075415774).jpg by Raita Futo, CC BY 2.0
Mutekiya

★ 3.6 · Tabelog, 2,000+ reviews

Rich pork-bone (tonkotsu) ramen with thick slices of roast pork. Only 17 counter seats, so expect a line, and it closes when the soup runs out.

Open from 10:30, every day · about 10 min on foot from Animate

Map ↗ Details ↗Photo: example of the dish. Ikkousha tonkotsu bowl.jpg by Wesoree, CC BY-SA 4.0
Anpuku

★ 3.6 · Tabelog, 1,900+ reviews, Udon East 100 (2024)

Creative udon served like pasta. The famous bowl is carbonara udon in a bonito-dashi sauce. On the west side of the station. No reservations at lunch.

Lunch 11:00 to 15:00, closed only at New Year · about 12 min on foot from Animate

Map ↗ Details ↗Photo: example of the dish. Kama-age Udon.jpg by Lombroso, public domain
Yan, Sichuan home cooking

★ 3.5 · Tabelog, 1,600+ reviews

Sichuan food, known for dry tantan noodles (no soup) and handmade fried and boiled gyoza. It appeared in the TV show Kodoku no Gourmet. The spice can be made milder on request.

Weekday lunch 11:30 to 15:00 · about 14 min on foot from Animate
