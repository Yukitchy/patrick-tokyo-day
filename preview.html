Course A · Kanda, Ochanomizu and Daikanyama

Bows, guitars & metal

The most metal of the three. The bow shop first, then the guitar street and a whole basement of heavy metal records, then a slower walk in Daikanyama.

The day

  1. 10:00
    Your hotel lobbyI pick you up. Kanda is about 10 minutes away.
  2. 10:15
    Koyama Kyugu, Kanda main storeBow maker for over 200 years, with its own workshop for bows, arrows and gloves. You can hold the bows and ask the staff anything. This is the main stop, so we take our time.
  3. 11:30
    Walk to OchanomizuAbout 10 minutes on foot.
  4. 11:40
    Ochanomizu guitar streetGuitar shops one after another along Meidai-dori: new, used and vintage.
  5. 12:15
    Disk Union Hard Rock / Heavy Metal storeA basement shop by Ochanomizu station, only rock and metal. Japanese pressings with the paper obi, new and used.
  6. 13:00
    LunchSee the picks below. Both are a few minutes from the record shop.
  7. 13:50
    Train to DaikanyamaAbout 40 minutes. The longest ride of the day.
  8. 14:30
    Daikanyama: Tsutaya Books and Log RoadQuiet streets, a big design bookshop and a garden path on an old rail line.
  9. 15:30
    DoneAt Daikanyama station, or I ride back with you.

The route

Hotel → Kanda about 10 min. Kanda → Ochanomizu 10 min on foot. Ochanomizu → Daikanyama about 40 min by train. Open the route in Google Maps ↗
