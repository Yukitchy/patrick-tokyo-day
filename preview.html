Tokyo · Tuesday, October 13

Bows first, then pick your noise.

Every course starts at Kanda Myojin shrine and Koyama Kyugu and then goes to Ikebukuro, the district for anime, manga and Pokémon. What goes around it is up to you. The metal bars open in the evening, so that part is optional.

Pickup is at your hotel lobby (Mitsui Garden Hotel Nihonbashi Premier) at 9:00. The bow shop opens at 10:00, so we start with a walk around Kanda Myojin shrine.

Course A · Kanda and Ikebukuro

Bows & anime

The anime course. The shrine and the bow shop first, then Ikebukuro: the big Animate store, spicy ramen, and the Pokémon Center in Sunshine City. Everything is open by the time we arrive.

The day

  1. 9:00
    Pickup at your hotel lobbyConfirmed for 9:00. Mitsukoshimae station is right next to the hotel.
  2. 9:15
    Train to Kanda MyojinGinza Line from Mitsukoshimae to Suehirocho, about 5 minutes, then a short walk.
  3. 9:30
    Kanda Myojin shrineAn old Edo shrine above Akihabara, popular with anime fans. We walk the grounds in the quiet before the bow shop opens.
  4. 10:00
    Walk to Koyama KyuguAbout 15 minutes on foot, downhill.
  5. 10:15
    Koyama Kyugu, Kanda main storeBow maker for over 200 years, with its own workshop for bows, arrows and gloves. It opens at 10:00. You can hold the bows and ask the staff anything. This is the main stop, so we take our time.
  6. 11:00
    Train to IkebukuroMarunouchi Line from Awajicho station, one minute from the bow shop. About 17 minutes, no changes.
  7. 11:30
    Animate Ikebukuro main storeThe flagship store of Japan's biggest anime shop chain. About 5 minutes on foot from the east exit. Manga, character goods, and the streets around it are full of anime shops.
  8. 12:30
    Lunch at Kikanbo IkebukuroSpicy miso ramen with chili and Sichuan pepper. You can ask for it milder.
  9. 13:15
    Walk to Sunshine CityDown Sunshine 60 Street, a long shopping street of anime, game and manga shops. About 15 minutes at a slow pace.
  10. 13:30
    Pokémon Center Mega TokyoOn the second floor of the Alpa shopping area in Sunshine City. The biggest Pokémon store in Tokyo.
  11. 14:00
    DoneIn Ikebukuro. Back to Nihonbashi is about 30 minutes: Marunouchi Line to Otemachi, then one stop on the Hanzomon Line. Or we split here.

The route

Hotel → Kanda Myojin about 5 min on the Ginza Line plus a short walk. Awajicho → Ikebukuro about 17 min on the Marunouchi Line. In Ikebukuro everything is on foot. Open the route in Google Maps ↗

Where we eat

Kikanbo IkebukuroIkebukuro · spicy miso ramenRed miso broth with chili and Sichuan pepper. You pick how hot and how numbing. Open 11:00 to 21:30.Open in Google Maps ↗

Good for The most anime of the three, and only one long train ride.

Keep in mind Kikanbo is spicy, but you can ask for it milder. The metal bars in Ikebukuro open at 19:00, after this plan ends.

Bows, records & metal
